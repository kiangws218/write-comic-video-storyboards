# Cinematic camera, light, and finish

Read after grounding and performance planning when cinematic finish is requested. This module changes presentation only; it never authorizes a new fact, composition anchor, action result, or relationship.

## Visual baseline

Resolve backing authority under `core-authoring.md` before polishing it. For omitted scenery, graphic emotion fields or background palette/effects, read `background-design.md`; mood grading does not replace a continuous environment. Its non-diegetic graphic effects are distinct from the physical atmosphere below.

Use supplied references first. Otherwise choose at most one work, one studio, and one director-style reference only when each clarifies a compatible target. Translate names into a few executable properties—2D acting density, line/background treatment, warm-cool contrast, depth, or light behavior—and state the stack once.

## Camera and rhythm

Decide the shot's visual purpose before selecting movement. Compare useful moving and fixed presentations from the actual beat rather than accepting the easiest source-safe setup. Push/pull can concentrate or release attention; pan/tilt/follow can trace an established task, path or height relation; an evidenced arc or rising/descending move can develop power, scale or a shared relation. Select a readable extent and rhythm, not a universal `轻推/微移`. Fixed framing remains useful for a held reaction, contrast, awkward pause or fragile contact. Do not impose either a fixed-camera default or an episode-wide movement quota, and do not add actor actions merely to justify moving the camera.

Before rendering, record a compact `camera_decision` in the existing shot entry: `mode`, `reason`, `anchor_phase`, `opening`, `landing`, and `path_basis` for a moving path. Use one coherent primary intention, not necessarily one movement verb: an arc with a gradual rise can be one continuous trajectory. Restore the comic composition at the declared middle/contact/result/endpoint, not necessarily throughout the shot; design `opening` separately under core authoring, never as a raw-comic first-frame lock. A 2D reframe traverses established image content. A viewpoint-changing trajectory requires inspected adjacent spatial views or supplied character/environment references for every newly exposed decision-changing surface/relation; record those references in `path_basis` and use the existing G review where needed. An unsupported full orbit is not admitted by calling it a camera move. Narrow only the unresolved path, choose another useful move or retain fixed framing; do not invent hidden people, geometry or surfaces. Preserve contact and framing needed to read the performance through the landing.

In a camera-only revision, edit `【镜头设计】` and its camera metadata/excerpts, not performance beats, dialogue, timing, light or sound. Record the camera landing separately from the performer's unchanged endpoint. At sequence review, repeated fixed framing prompts a purpose check, not an automatic failure; intentional stillness remains valid. A moving shot with no evidenced path or useful landing should be fixed instead.

Choose terms only when they change the image:

- lens/scale and function: wide for depth, normal for natural relations, telephoto for compression/intimacy, macro/long lens for supported detail;
- camera height/direction, optical axis, horizon/eye line;
- subject placement, negative space, leading line/vanishing point, foreground occluder, layered depth;
- non-contradictory focal plane or supported focus transfer;
- one coherent camera trajectory—fixed, push/pull, pan/tilt, follow, supported arc/orbit, rise/descent, or motivated handheld—with useful extent/rhythm and readable opening/landing.

When the camera moves during character performance, state what initiates the move, what relation or feature it follows, and where it settles. `小幅横移/轻推` alone is incomplete; the landing must preserve the performer or prop endpoint rather than merely naming a direction.

Camera clarifies action, attention, space, relation, emotion, or result; it cannot substitute for performance. A cited composition remains recognizable at its declared phase. A separately cut viewpoint is justified coverage. A continuous admitted trajectory may change angle/elevation within one numbered shot; it does not trigger the added-shot contrast gate at each intermediate frame or become a hidden cut. Actual cuts still need separate numbers and durations.

Keep formal camera prose executable: the useful framing, movement/path, extent or speed profile, final framing and any meaningful focus progression. Do not append `让关系读清楚/把关注集中于/确保完整可读/呈现源格关系` or repeat the same crop as a purported landing. Put purpose, source matching, safety and review explanations in `camera_decision`/the existing audit. A fixed shot normally needs no separate landing paragraph; moving shots need a concrete final frame, not `最终清楚呈现`. This is a meaning-based edit, not a ban on `保留/清楚/焦点`: actual crop limits, focus planes and important occlusion remain executable information.

Plan acting before an optional tightening of the view. Preserve visible shoulders, torso, existing gesture and partner response when they carry its readable change. Do not repeatedly select face, back-of-head, hair or ear inserts just because their geometry is easy or their subject differs from the next panel: a coverage difference is necessary but not a dramatic purpose. A supported detail insert can reveal pressure, hesitation or information; a source-imposed facial crop can carry clear facial acting. Choose from those actual functions rather than forcing wider shots or camera motion.

For continuous action use temporal contrast: dense small changes/steady camera for anticipation; wider pose spacing and directional follow for acceleration; concentrated in-betweens for grip/balance/mechanics; selective slow motion only at supported realization/apex/near-contact; compact contact treatment; proportional response; readable settled result. Keep one intention and consequence together when duration allows.

## Lighting and materials

Derive light from the environment and people positions. State source, direction, occlusion, main/fill or reflection, shadow boundary/depth, motivated rim/highlight, and local material response. Light may evolve with supported movement but cannot erase a face, hand, contact, or anchor.

Connect light to visible material: hair translucency; skin/eye catchlight; cloth folds/compression; diffuse wood grain; metal edge reflection; glass reflection/refraction; wet reflection; crystal transmission; steam/haze scattering. Direction and decay follow the source.

Atmosphere is conditional:

- 丁达尔光: directional light plus haze/steam/dust and an opening;
- 微尘/薄雾: plausible particulate or atmospheric source;
- 镜头炫光: bright source near/crossing the optical axis, brief;
- 轮廓光: side/back source following the silhouette;
- bloom/glow/overexposure: local peak followed by decay.

Use only effects with a job; do not stack them by habit.

A graphic star, flat stripe or soft-focus color patch in an admitted emotion field is not a physical light source, fog bank or magic event. It needs dramatic purpose and a researched visual form, not invented weather or automatic light spill onto the performer. Physical atmosphere still needs the visible cause above.

## Effects and finish gate

An effect has onset, peak, interaction, and decay beside its visible cause. Align speed treatment with the established vector. Use one dominant impact treatment and material-specific response: concrete chips/heavy dust, metal spark/highlight, glass shards, water sheet/droplets/mist/rings. Reserve the largest response for the supported peak.

Confirm source composition/facts remain recognizable; camera has one reason; focus/depth agree; light/atmosphere have a source; material response is local/directional; effects peak and decay; added coverage does not duplicate the next source shot; and polish has not replaced acting, dialogue readability, or the endpoint.
