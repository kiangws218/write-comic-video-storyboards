# Regression cases for skill maintenance

Read only when modifying or forward-testing this skill. These cases protect behavior; do not turn each case into a duplicated rule.

| Case | Required result |
|---|---|
| A 3-second prompt and its 6-second answer occupy different consecutive panels in the same exchange | Prefer one 9-second clip with two numbered, separately referenced shots. Keeping each panel's complete coverage together does not require one exclusive clip per panel. |
| Consecutive source panels need different angles or scales within one supported action/reaction | Start numbered shots at camera changes, retain their distinct source anchors, and group them into the same clip when natural timing fits. Shot boundary is not clip boundary. |
| A one-to-three-panel inspection batch ends before the continuous scene beat ends | Carry the tentative clip boundary forward; batching is not editing. Check the immediate next unit before finalizing, without reconstructing source evidence from prose. |
| Two short adjacent clips fit the 15-second ceiling but contain a true scene/time break or deliberate dramatic landing | A duration-only hint prompts semantic review, not compulsory merging. Record the concrete retain reason internally; no minimum clip duration or padding. |
| The user explicitly caps clips at 10 seconds while the skill's default hard ceiling is 15 | Apply 10 as the actual ceiling. A naturally over-limit single-panel unit may use the recorded stable overflow split; no individual clip may exceed 10, and no accelerated speech or thinned acting. |
| 第16話_04_001 contains two road-control explanations plus ordering food and a reply, naturally about 18 seconds | Preserve every owner/turn and necessary acting; split only at a complete thought/turn or stable state into consecutive clips each at most 15 seconds. Record overflow_split internally, cite the full source once, and continue the actual pose/contact rather than replaying entry. A compatible following reaction may join the split tail. |
| A 9-second panel unit is split for convenience and given overflow_split metadata | Fail the exception: both the natural estimate and actual panel-owned coverage must exceed the applicable ceiling; a label cannot authorize ordinary fragmentation. |
| An overflow split inserts unrelated panel coverage between its two parts, omits the continuation state or lists the wrong clips | Fail the recorded exception. Ownership must be contiguous, clip numbers exact and consecutive, with a concrete boundary and continuation state. |
| A valid overflow_split exists but one resulting clip exceeds the ceiling | Fail that clip. This exception changes panel-unit ownership only, never the per-video ceiling. |
| Ordinary action authoring loads only the default core/dialogue modules, with no D/P/G flags | Still classify the physical beat, declare the anchor phase and assess supported temporal development before prose. No compatibility-module or P-card dependency for action design. |
| 第9話_24_002 shows 春子's anatomical right index finger at her right ear, with no adjacent panel drawing the lift and no established ongoing gesture | Include the short right-hand lift/entry from screen-left/lower-left into the depicted ear contact, then compatible local finger/wrist motion. Restore B; do not open already ear-picking, invent a tool/pocket search, reverse hands or require two drawn poses. |
| 第9話_19_001 is 青's face close-up and 第9話_19_002 initiates her embrace of 春子 | Admit a short source-compatible approach from the left and arm closure into the depicted two-person anchor. Missing drawn approach is not a rejection reason; do not add a run-up/jump/detour or an unsupported release. |
| A preceding panel already shows the same finger at the same ear or the same sustained embrace | Continue the ongoing action/contact; do not replay lifting or approaching just to satisfy an A-phase default. |
| A necessary hand-entry or approach is admitted from one source anchor | Keep observed B facts in source_facts and mark the undrawn lead-in as inferred in existing action admissions/motion_decision; do not claim it was visibly drawn or create a second ledger. |
| Neighboring views preserve position, facing and contact; only the camera or speaking subject changes | Choose a direct cut. An ordinary speaker/listener reverse is not a physical gap and does not require a bridge or new gesture. |
| A small admitted head turn/hand lift can connect the previous endpoint and next opening within their existing phrases | Coordinate outgoing C and incoming A, preserving both source anchors and showing the change once. Do not repeat the preparation at the next shot or relabel B as a first-frame A. |
| Inspected views establish a person outside a known curtained threshold and then inside; the opening/access and ownership are clear but crossing is omitted | Admit the shortest opening/crossing action in separately numbered uncited coverage when the main shots cannot carry it cleanly. Prefer a contrasting action detail if it explains the crossing; use a supported wider view if required. Keep inference out of observed facts and group the bridge with the continuous beat within the ceiling. |
| Two endpoints are in different locations but no inspected geometry establishes their connecting route | Do not invent a walk/door/vehicle route or claim a static cutaway repairs the gap. Narrow to supported content, use a justified intentional ellipsis, or report the missing evidence. |
| A proposed bridge rotates or pulls a jointly held object while sustained mouth/hand contact must continue | Reject that incompatible motion. Preserve ownership/contact, select another evidenced change or direct cut; an insert has no compulsory movement quota. |
| A speaker raises the left palm, coverage cuts to a listener, then returns to the speaker without an evidenced lowering | Return with the left palm already raised and continue the supported task. Cold-start restatement is not another lift; the listener cut does not reset physical state. |
| A bridge has one coverage owner but its admitted hand/target facts and action evidence come from both inspected neighboring endpoints | Use evidence_images for the two endpoints and keep derived_from singular. Exact observed claims and action bindings may use either endpoint; borrowing evidence cannot trigger extra coverage ownership. |
| A bridge belongs to the incoming panel rather than the outgoing one | Contrast it with the actual preceding view and the later endpoint composition, not the panel after its owner. Evidence and ownership do not decide the same boundary. |
| Evidence images are unregistered, uninspected, typography-only, duplicate, nonadjacent, or supplied on ordinary/source-locked coverage | Reject the evidence expansion; preserve the existing allowlist and ownership checks. This is not an episode-wide fact pool. |
| Acting or coverage revisions change a formerly approved outgoing pose/contact or incoming opening | Recheck the affected boundary under core's gate and record concrete states/connection in the existing audit; an early gap check or generic source-safe marker does not settle the revised cut. |
| A hand-only insert pushes a curtain while the next shot claims the whole body already crossed its threshold | Review actual visibility/space; do not call the crossing enacted merely because admission booleans and structural checks pass. Reframe to show the necessary transition or narrow/replan it. |
| A clip opening correctly restates a continued raised hand/contact | Preserve continuation rather than neutralizing the pose; self-contained wording is not a physical reset. |
| A hand starts below the crop and becomes the already established hand at the anchor | Allow visibility count to change during entry, with ownership/side and final count preserved; do not invent the off-screen elbow support, a second hand or a pocket action. |
| The current action image plus an adjacent same-axis phase visibly establishes a foot descending to the depicted contact | Admit the evidenced descent into the contact anchor when framing permits; do not start already planted and replace the descent with dust alone. Do not add another step, unseen supporting foot or route. |

| The execution mode would bind the comic reference as the first frame | Reject that asset/mode combination. Use a compatible reference workflow or a separately approved opening asset when authorized; otherwise report the execution blocker. Do not delete admitted A or relabel B as A. |
| A comic shows preparation or an already ongoing/held state | Classify its physical phase faithfully and preserve continuity. Do not invent extra events or replay a completed lead-in merely to make the opening look different; an incidental pose similarity is not a first-frame asset lock. |

| Physical B/C phases and P-card B/C labels occur in the same plan | Keep physical-phase selection separate from semantic beats/coupling; a populated P-card does not complete the action-phase decision. |
| A supplied named character sheet matches the current monochrome crop by ear shape, hair silhouette and costume construction | Use its canonical name consistently in all fields and speaker mappings; preserve the comic's current pose/props and explicit output-style direction. A monochrome input alone does not require monochrome output. |
| A character resembles two sheets only by dark hair, or a tiny silhouette hides distinguishing traits | Keep a stable original/role label and record uncertainty; do not force a name from story continuity. |

| A previously approved shot receives only punctuation or numbering fixes | Run mechanical checks without reopening images or claiming a new visual audit; a later crop/identity/contact edit reopens only affected evidence. |

| Listener is absent from the cited speaker composition | Use an uncited reverse shot or hard-cut sub-shot; do not pull the listener into the cited frame. |
| Character looks upward in a high-camera view | Determine angle from perspective; do not label it low-angle because of gaze. |
| Subject is left-third or side-view | Preserve placement and viewing direction as separate facts; do not default to centered/front. |
| A two-person cited crop has one large foreground subject on the left, a partly occluded subject behind on the right, and a prop extending rightward | Trigger the compact composition lock; preserve screen-left/right, depth, overlap, and prop direction. Never rearrange subjects by speaking order, narrative importance, or inferred world-space direction. |
| A floor-level crop looks through a standing character's legs while two large object halves fill both foreground sides | Trigger the unusual-viewpoint composition lock; preserve the between-leg camera, near/far hierarchy, and visible object placement instead of rebuilding an ordinary distant full shot. |
| A close-up shows one hand gripping one cord connected to one oval moving end against a white background | Trigger the topology lock; keep count, connections, gripping hand and moving end. Resolve the white backing under core's authority gate: preserve deliberate graphic staging, or admit only bounded, inspected environment continuity when scenery was omitted. White alone does not decide intent. No second hand or repeated beads. |
| A cited comic composition occurs midway or at the endpoint | Reference that phase directly; do not force it to be the opening frame or a static hold. |
| Adjacent panels explicitly show compression followed by launch | The phases may be connected in one continuous shot with both references in order; do not add circling, landing, or another attack. |
| Two adjacent numbered shots formally cite the same dialogue-panel reference, even after changing focal length or cropping to one eye | Fail validation. First preserve ordered semantic turns. Keep only the full source-matching shot cited and turn required later beats into uncited coverage that changes at least two meaningful dimensions; merge only a duplicate with no independent turn. A small zoom, focus transfer or eye-only crop is not a new source-locked shot. |
| An uncited coverage shot differs from the preceding shot but recreates the next comic panel's dominant subject, scale, axis and placement | Redesign or merge it before drafting. Every added view must differ on at least two meaningful composition dimensions from both the previous planned shot and the next source composition; lookahead is part of first-pass coverage planning. |
| A panel contains Korean/Japanese/Chinese SFX glyphs, speech balloons, captions, labels or page furniture | Record them only in `page_overlays`. Never render their glyphs, language, shape or position. Map an SFX to concrete sound; if no non-typographic visual remains, attach the sound to an adjacent visible shot and omit a standalone visual shot. |
| A below-limit panel ends in one clip and its added coverage starts the next for convenience | Fail the panel-ownership audit; retain its full coverage together. A naturally over-limit unit instead requires core's recorded overflow split. In either case, later coverage must differ meaningfully, not merely by focal length, focus or foreground size. |
| A clip ends with an uncited reaction using the same character, side close-up, dominant cup, and dramatic function as the next clip's first comic panel | Compare across the clip boundary. Remove or merge the reaction, or change at least two meaningful dimensions such as subject focus plus scale or viewpoint plus visible information. |
