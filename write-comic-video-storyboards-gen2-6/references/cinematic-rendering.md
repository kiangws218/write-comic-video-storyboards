# Cinematic rendering and finish

Use this module after source grounding and performance planning. It controls how a supported panel or action is photographed and rendered; it never authorizes a new character, prop, location, action result, or relationship. `source-grounding.md` remains authoritative.

## Global visual baseline

At scene or task level, a user-specified or deliberately selected work, animation studio, or director-style name may be used as a compact visual prior. When names are not supplied, choose at most one work, one studio, and one director-style reference only when each materially clarifies a source-compatible target. Treat names as rendering references: preserve the project's characters, designs, story facts, and source composition, and state the few executable properties that matter (for example: dense 2D acting, restrained warm-cool contrast, layered painted backgrounds). Do not rely on names alone, and do not repeat the same stack in every shot.

## Camera and space

Choose one primary camera behavior per actual shot: locked, restrained push/pull, pan, follow, or motivated handheld response. State only terms that change the image:

- **Lens and axis:** wide/normal/telephoto or an equivalent focal-length feel; camera height, optical axis, and horizon/eye line.
- **Composition:** leading lines, vanishing point, foreground occluder, negative space, near-large/far-small scale, and foreground/midground/background separation.
- **Depth:** space compression for telephoto intimacy; expanded depth for wide spatial clarity; shallow or deep focus only when it serves attention and remains compatible with 2D layout.
- **Focus:** locked focal plane, rack/focus transfer between supported subjects, and a clear start/end focus state. Do not combine contradictory focus instructions.
- **Motion:** establish a readable opening and landing frame (`起幅`/`落幅`); keep one motivated movement rather than stacking unrelated moves.

Examples: `35mm广角，视平线略低，前景门框作遮挡，近大远小的走廊引导线将视线带向人物`；`85mm压缩前后空间，焦平面锁在双眼，句末焦点后移到手中物件`。 These are optional clauses, not a mandatory checklist.

## Light, atmosphere, and material

Name the source, direction, and visible response: window/overhead/practical/backlight, side light, split lighting, edge/rim light, highlight band, shadow depth, or warm-cool contrast. Add atmosphere only when the environment supports it:

- `丁达尔光束` requires directional light through haze, steam, dust, or a visible opening;
- `环境薄雾` and `空气微尘` require an atmospheric or particulate source;
- `镜头炫光` requires a bright source near or crossing the optical axis and should be brief;
- `轮廓光` requires a back/side source and must follow the silhouette;
- overexposure, bloom, and glow must decay and must not erase faces, hands, contact, or source anchors.

Use these effects selectively, with at most the few clauses that have a job in the current shot. Do not make every shot contain beams, rim light, dust, and flare. Connect light to materials: hair translucency, skin and eye catchlight, cloth compression/folds, wood grain diffuse response, metal edge reflection, glass reflection/refraction, wet-surface reflection, or steam scattering. The response follows the established light direction and fades with the cause.

## Compact shot recipe

Prefer:

`景别/焦段与轴线 → 构图和层次 → 起幅/运镜/焦点 → 受支持的表演与动作 → 光源与局部材质反应 → 适量空气效果 → 落幅和结束状态`

Do not append generic `电影感/高级质感/高质量` as a substitute for a concrete camera, light, depth, or material decision. When a cinematic clause changes angle, subject placement, overlap, contact, or endpoint, remove it or classify the view as a separately justified supported/uncited phase.

## Finish audit

Before emitting a shot, check: (1) the cited composition and source facts remain recognizable; (2) camera motion has one reason; (3) focus and depth terms do not conflict; (4) light and atmospheric effects have an in-scene source; (5) material responses are local and directional; (6) effects peak and decay instead of becoming a permanent overlay; and (7) visual polish has not replaced acting, dialogue readability, or the real endpoint.
